# Catwalk 21

Language for the salon's public website.

## Language

**Branch**:
A physical Catwalk 21 salon location with its own address and contact details. The current website lists Thakur Complex and Thakur Village.
_Avoid_: Store, office

**Service**:
A type of salon or spa treatment offered to visitors. The current website groups services as Salon, Spa, Skin care, and Bridal.
_Avoid_: Product
